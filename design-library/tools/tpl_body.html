Singapore standard designs: pick an approved design, build the tender pack, send the details to AutoCAD.

Structure

Shows the smallest approved design that meets or exceeds the requirement and passes the Singapore checks. Designs awaiting approval, and designs that fail a check, are listed separately.

Copy CAD export gives the same text as the CAD_Export sheet: paste it into Notepad, save as .txt, then run TPSHEETS in AutoCAD. Only Approved designs are placed. Quantities are preliminary for tender estimation; QS to verify.
